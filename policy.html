Gym Policy

Welcome to Sigma Fitness. To ensure a safe, respectful, and premium gym environment for everyone, all members must follow the rules below. Joining the gym means you agree to all of these policies. Please read carefully.

₹200 Refundable Demo Deposit

A security deposit of ₹200 is required for a demo session. This amount will be fully refunded if you decide not to join Sigma Fitness after your demo. If you proceed to join, this amount may be adjusted toward your first month's fee.

No Damage to Equipment

Members are strictly not allowed to break, misuse, or damage any gym equipment. Gym equipment is expensive and must be handled with care. Any damage caused will be the full financial responsibility of the member who caused it.

No Items to be Taken Home

Absolutely no gym equipment, accessories, or any item belonging to Sigma Fitness may be taken outside the premises — not during gym hours, not after closing. All items must remain inside the gym at all times.

Advance Monthly Fee Payment

All members must pay their monthly membership fee of ₹600 in advance at the beginning of each month. Failure to pay on time may result in suspension of gym access until payment is cleared.

Under CCTV Surveillance

The entire Sigma Fitness premises is under 24/7 CCTV surveillance. All activities inside and around the gym are being recorded continuously. This is for the safety and security of all members and staff.

Strict Penalty for Theft or Misconduct

If any member is caught taking gym property outside the premises — whether during or after gym hours — they will be required to pay a penalty fine AND return the item immediately. Repeat offences may lead to permanent ban from the gym. No exceptions will be made.

By joining Sigma Fitness you agree to all the above policies.
